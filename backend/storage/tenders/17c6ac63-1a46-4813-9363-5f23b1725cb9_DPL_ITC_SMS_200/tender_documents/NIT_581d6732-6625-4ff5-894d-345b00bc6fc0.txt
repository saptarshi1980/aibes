ANNEXURE-I: Scope of Work and Technical Specification

Scope of Work:
a. Objective
The objective of this tender is to empanel/ select a service provider for providing SMS
Gateway Services for delivery of Transactional and Service SMS messages generated from
the existing applications through secure API integration. The selected bidder shall provide a
robust, scalable, secure, and TRAI-compliant SMS platform for transmission of SMS
messages to subscribers of all telecom operators across India.
b. Scope of Services
The selected bidder shall:
i) SMS Gateway Services
• Provide SMS gateway connectivity for sending Transactional and Service SMS
messages.
• Facilitate delivery of SMS to all telecom operators in India.
• Provide web-based portal and API-based access for SMS transmission.
• Ensure high availability and uninterrupted service throughout the contract period.
ii) API Integration
• Provide REST API, HTTP API, HTTPS API, SMPP or equivalent integration
interfaces.
• Support integration with existing applications without requiring major modifications.
• Provide API documentation, sample code, technical support, and testing environment.
• Support OTP, alerts, notifications, acknowledgements, status updates, and system-
generated messages.
iii) DLT Compliance
• Support registration and management of:
o Principal Entity (PE)
o Sender Headers
o Content Templates
o PE-TM Binding
• Ensure compliance with TRAI regulations and amendments issued from time to time.
• Facilitate onboarding and maintenance activities on DLT platforms.
iv) Message Delivery and Reporting
• Provide real-time delivery reports (DLR).
• Provide message status tracking.
• Provide downloadable reports in Excel/ CSV/ PDF formats.
• Maintain detailed logs of SMS transactions.
v) Technical Support
• The Durgapur Projects Limited (DPL) is/will be registered as a Principal Entity (PE)
on the DLT platform. The successful bidder shall assist DPL in PE-TM binding,
header registration, template registration, and all activities necessary for compliance
with TRAI’s DLT framework.
• Provide helpdesk support during business hours and emergency support on 24×7
basis.
• Nominate dedicated account manager and technical support engineer.
• Resolve service issues within stipulated SLA timelines.
